09 — FAQ

Questionsfounders ask.

What is STARTUP?

A launchpad for AI startups on pump.fun. You describe a startup in one sentence, an AI builds the company (name, ticker, tagline, pitch) and a fictional AI founder with a face, and you launch its coin from your own wallet. Every startup gets its own page, a founder who talks, funding rounds read live from the chart, and a Studio where the founder films videos with Higgsfield.

What does a launch cost?

pump.fun's own launch cost (about 0.02 SOL), plus any dev buy you choose and PumpPortal's 0.5% fee on that buy, plus the network fee for the split transaction. STARTUP adds no fee of its own.

Where do the creator fees go?

Each coin's pump.fun creator fees are split three ways: 30% to the burn wallet, which buys and burns $STARTUP; 35% to the runway wallet, which pays for that startup's Studio videos; 35% to the founder wallet that launched it.

Can anyone change the split later?

No. The split is written with pump.fun's fee sharing in the second transaction of the launch, and that same transaction revokes the admin. After that pump.fun refuses any change, from the founder or from us. The split and its lock are read straight from chain on every startup page.

Is the founder a real person?

No. Founders are AI-generated and fictional, and they are labelled AI everywhere they appear. Ideas about real people are refused.

How does the Studio work?

The founder wallet picks a format and signs a short message. Higgsfield turns the founder's headshot into a short video. Every order goes into a ledger, and there is a daily cap per startup and across the whole pad.

What are the funding rounds?

Labels for how far a startup has come, read live from its market cap: Garage at launch, Seed at $25K, IPO when it graduates off the pump.fun curve, Unicorn at $1M. They are not real funding.

Is this equity?

No. These are memecoins, not shares. Holding one gives you no ownership, no revenue and no rights. Memecoins can go to zero. Nothing here is financial advice.

Is STARTUP part of pump.fun or Higgsfield?

No. STARTUP is an independent site. It launches coins through pump.fun and makes videos through Higgsfield's public API.

Which wallets work?

Phantom, Solflare and Backpack. You approve the launch once; both transactions are in that one prompt.

STARTUP is independent and not affiliated with pump.fun or Higgsfield. Founders are AI-generated and fictional. Tokens launched here are memecoins with no intrinsic value or expectation of financial return, not equity or securities. Memecoins can go to zero. Nothing on this site is financial advice.
