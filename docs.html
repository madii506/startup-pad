Overview

STARTUP is a pump.fun launchpad where every coin is an AI startup. One sentence becomes a company (name, ticker, tagline, pitch) and a fictional AI founder with a headshot, a bio and a voice. The coin launches from the founder's own wallet, its creator fees are split and locked at launch, and the startup gets a page, a talking founder, live funding rounds and a Studio.

The launch

  1. Kit. /api/kit turns your sentence into the company and founder with an AI model. You edit anything.
  2. Visuals. /api/art makes a flat logo and a photoreal founder headshot (Higgsfield Soul when connected, otherwise FLUX). You can upload your own instead.
  3. Upload. The logo and coin metadata go to pump.fun's IPFS. The founder record (headshot + kit) is uploaded the same way, so the startup page can be rebuilt from chain and IPFS alone.
  4. Transaction 1 creates the coin on pump.fun (built by PumpPortal) with your optional dev buy. Your browser makes the new mint key; it never leaves the page.
  5. Transaction 2 creates the coin's pump.fun fee-sharing config, writes the split and revokes the admin, and carries the STARTUP registry memo.

Both transactions are signed in one wallet prompt and sent in order. If the second one doesn't land in time, the launch page asks you to approve it once more.

Fee split & lock

pump.fun pays a creator fee on every trade. STARTUP routes it with pump.fun's own fee sharing (program pfeeUxB6jkeY1Hxd7CsFCAjcbHA9rWtchMGdZ6VojVZ):

  • 30% burn to the burn wallet, which buys $STARTUP and burns it.
  • 35% runway to the runway wallet, which pays only for Studio videos.
  • 35% founder to the wallet that launched the coin.

The split is written by update_fee_shares_v2 in the launch, which revokes the config admin. After that pump.fun rejects every change (sharingConfigAdminRevoked). Every startup page reads the config from chain and shows the shares and whether it is locked.

Fees collect in the coin's creator vault until someone presses Pay out, which calls pump.fun's permissionless distribute instruction. Anyone can press it.

Burn

The burn wallet's SOL is used for one thing: buying $STARTUP on the market and burning it. Burns are read from chain and listed with their transactions.

Runway & Studio policy

The Studio makes short videos with Higgsfield's API (image-to-video from the founder headshot). Rules enforced on the server:

  • Only the startup's founder wallet can order, by signing a short message that expires in five minutes.
  • Daily cap per startup and a daily cap across the whole pad (shown on the Studio page).
  • Every order is written to a ledger with its cost estimate and result.
  • Founders are always labelled AI. No real people.

Funding rounds

Rounds are labels read live from the market: Garage at launch, Seed at $25K market cap, IPO when the coin graduates off the pump.fun bonding curve, Unicorn at $1M. They are not real funding and they unlock nothing financial. Founder videos filmed in the office format use the setting of the current round.

Registry

There is no database of launches. Every launch's split transaction carries the memo su:v1:<mint>:<founderCid> and the read-only registry key …, so anyone can list every STARTUP launch with getSignaturesForAddress on that key. The founder CID points to the founder record on IPFS.

Risks & independence

Memecoins are volatile and can go to zero. A STARTUP coin is not equity, gives no ownership, revenue or rights, and comes with no promise of returns. Founders are fictional and AI-generated. STARTUP is independent and not affiliated with pump.fun or Higgsfield. Smart contracts and third-party services (pump.fun, PumpPortal, Higgsfield, IPFS gateways) can fail. Only launch with funds you can afford to lose.
